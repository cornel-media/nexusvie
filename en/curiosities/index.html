Curiosities

What science and technology are teaching us this week.

  • AI Curiosity · Nov 3, 2026
    Describe a protein in words, and AI designs it

    Researchers at Westlake University in China built Pinal, an AI that proposes proteins from a plain sentence such as “a protein that glows green.” Lab results are encouraging but modest: of 29 glowing candidates tested, only one actually lit up.

  • AI Curiosity · Nov 3, 2026
    Spotting a dangerous bacterium before anyone falls ill

    Researchers at the Technical University of Denmark developed PathogenFinder2, an AI that estimates whether a still-unknown bacterium could cause disease. Trained on more than 21,000 genomes, it could scan samples such as wastewater to raise alerts earlier.

  • AI Curiosity · Nov 2, 2026
    250,000 stones tell the story of an ancient village

    At Shivta in the Negev desert, University of Haifa researchers photographed the ruins by drone and let an AI measure every stone, one by one. They traced how the village grew between the 2nd and 6th centuries, when three churches were built there, and found a previously unknown ancient boundary.

  • AI Curiosity · Nov 3, 2026
    Weather outlooks a bit more reliable three to four weeks ahead

    A team including a University of Toronto researcher built a tool that learns from past forecast mistakes and corrects them. For three-to-four-week trends (warmer, cooler, drier, wetter), it doubled the skill of an AI weather model.
