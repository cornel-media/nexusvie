Meditation 06

Pause

November 7, 2026

“God saw everything that he had made, and, behold, it was very good.”

Genesis 1:31 · World English Bible

There is always something left to improve. A detail to correct, an instruction to add. We take over what someone has done, even when they are doing just fine. We want to help, but by stepping in again and again, we end up taking all the space.

In the creation story, God does more than act. Again and again, he looks at what he has just created and recognizes that it is good. These pauses give the story its rhythm: create, look, appreciate. Not everything calls at once for another intervention.

After the human being appears, God could have gone on. Multiplied the works, shown humanity the full extent of his power. Yet he stops. Not because his power is spent, but because his work is complete. We can read in this rest a restraint he chooses: God does not occupy all the space. He leaves his creation room to live and to grow.

The Sabbath thus opens a space where we can live together without taking up all the space. We stop acting on the other person so we can be present with them, let them breathe, make their own choices, and help them flourish. I can be there without directing everything. Support without deciding in their place.

It is also a question addressed to the churches: when rules and control stifle the person, do they not contradict this rest of God that makes room for life? A community can accompany people without imposing on each one how they should grow. Letting go does not mean abandoning the other person, but giving up holding them so tightly that they can no longer move forward.

Today, to whom can we leave more room? Let’s listen to that person without deciding for them what their next step should be.
