Meditation 09

Too Many Tabs Open

November 10, 2026

“Martha, Martha, thou art careful and troubled about many things: But one thing is needful.”

Luke 10:41-42 · KJV

There are days when the computer has twenty tabs open, and the mind roughly as many. An email to finish, a notification blinking, a grocery list half written. We move from one to the next without really being anywhere.

In a village, Jesus is received in the home of two sisters. Martha is busy in the kitchen, Mary sits down and listens. When Martha complains, the answer is not a harsh rebuke: he calls her twice by name, gently, and reminds her that only one thing truly matters at that moment.

I often recognize myself in Martha. Not because serving would be wrong, but because I sometimes confuse being busy with being present. We can do a great deal for someone without ever listening to them.

Perhaps we do not need to close everything. Only to choose, today, the one thing that deserves our full attention: a conversation, a meal, a moment of silence. The rest can wait a few minutes.

One thing at a time, and often, that is enough.
