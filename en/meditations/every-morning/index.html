Meditation 03

Every Morning

“It is because of Yahweh’s loving kindnesses that we are not consumed, because his mercies don’t fail. They are new every morning. Great is your faithfulness.”

Lamentations 3:22-23 · World English Bible

Yesterday, nothing really worked. An email taken the wrong way. The bus missed by two minutes, slush up to my ankles. That night I ate standing up in the kitchen, barely noticing what was on the plate.

This morning it was still dark when I put the coffee on. Frost on the window, the radio turned low. Outside, the neighbour was already scraping his windshield. Nothing was solved, to be honest. But it was another day. You feel that in your body before you understand it.

This ancient text comes from a man who had lost everything. Not a poet sitting warm in his living room: his city lay in ruins. He had every reason to stay quiet. Instead he writes that kindness hasn’t run out, that it comes back every morning. I find that stubborn. In a good way.

We don’t have to settle yesterday before starting today. We can call the person back. Open the file where we left it. Take a ten-minute walk at lunch instead of eating in front of the screen. Talk to ourselves a little less harshly, too.

The day is coming up. We start again from where we are.
