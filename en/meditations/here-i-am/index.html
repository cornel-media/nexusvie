Meditation 05

Here I Am

“Abraham! And he said, Here I am.”

Genesis 22:1 · World English Bible

The phone buzzes while we’re talking. A message lands during supper. We say “yes” while looking somewhere else. We’re in the room, but our mind is already elsewhere — tomorrow, the list, what’s still missing.

In ancient Hebrew there’s a word for the opposite of that: hineni. It’s usually translated “here I am.” Not only “I’m here.” More like “I’m here for you, now.”

Abraham answers that way three times in the same story: to God, to his son, to the angel. Each time, before he knows what comes next. Not after he understands everything. Before.

We often do the reverse. We want the plan before we say yes. We keep a margin, just in case. That’s human. But something is lost when we’re never really present — not for the person in front of us, and not for ourselves.

Before the next automatic “yes,” we can pause for a second. Put the phone down. Lift our eyes. Say, simply: here I am.

Presence begins where we stop negotiating our place.
