Privacy

This page explains how Nexus uses the information you provide when subscribing to the newsletter.

Information collected

We ask for your email address and your preferred language (French or English). Brevo may also retain the date and record of your subscription and confirmation.

How it is used

This information is used only to send you meditations and news from Nexus in your chosen language. It is not sold. You can unsubscribe using the link in every email.

Service provider

Brevo manages subscriptions, mailing lists, and delivery for Nexus. Your information is shared with Brevo for that purpose and may be processed outside Quebec. See Brevo’s privacy policy as well.

Visitor statistics

To know how many people read the site and which pages interest them, Nexus uses Cloudflare Web Analytics. It sets no cookies, does not track visitors across sites, and produces only aggregate, anonymous statistics.

Your choices

To access, correct, or request deletion of your information, write to contact@nexusvie.com. You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link.
