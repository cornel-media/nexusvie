about

Cornel Serban. Pastor and Director of Communications & IT at the Quebec Conference of Seventh-day Adventists. I care about building tools that serve people. Born in Romania, with time spent in Spain and France, I have been based in Chambly since 2024.

Nexus is where I share what I create outside institutional structures — short meditations, a podcast, and small software tools. I am drawn to faith lived honestly, thought that takes its time, and technology that serves rather than distracts.
